# Faculty research snapshot

The roster remains the existing 149 people (official directory rows 4–152, excluding three leadership entries). Research records are joined by official email, never by display name. The directory contains two V. Gowri entries with different emails; their research records must remain separate.

Collected on 8 October 2026. All 149 names were checked against indexed official SRM faculty profiles for published Google Scholar links. Direct downloads of SRM PDFs failed, and Scholar author search required sign-in. Public profiles with known IDs were read in the browser.

Coverage: 26 verified public profiles; 116 without a retrievable unambiguous link; 3 published links without a readable public profile; 4 profiles requiring review because Scholar lists another institution. This is a partial import, not complete coverage of 149 faculty.

Each discovered record stores the official identity evidence URL, Scholar URL, observed Scholar name and affiliation, research interests, and citation metrics when displayed. Metrics are the all-time and “Since 2021” columns shown by Scholar on the collection date. Missing metrics remain null, not zero. Publications are linked on the original Scholar profile rather than copied into this dataset.

Only verified records are displayed as research data and included in research-interest search. Review records retain the observed candidate data for auditing but do not display it as confirmed faculty research. A different institution can reflect stale affiliation or a wrong identity; it needs evidence before confirmation.

To complete coverage, obtain the missing public Scholar profile URLs from faculty or accessible official profile PDFs, verify each against the official identity, then update the corresponding email record in faculty-research.json. Re-read metrics from Scholar and record the actual checkedOn date. Do not populate unresolved entries using name similarity or estimates.

The research snapshot is separate from faculty-data.json so scripts/import-faculty.mjs can regenerate the roster without overwriting research evidence.
